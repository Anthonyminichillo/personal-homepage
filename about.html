My Story

My Background

I'm currently pursuing my Master's in Computer Science at Northeastern University in Boston. My path into CS wasn't traditional. In 2020, COVID-19 pushed my life into a new direction. What was supposed to be a career in Aviation turned to me going to community college completely online. I did four years of school without any zooms or in-person interactions. This changed my life for the better, I took the opportunity to find my self through travel and life experience while simultaneously completing my bachelors degree in business. Five years later I decided to go back to the class room because now I am focused and ready for my career to start.

Work & Experience

Computer Science is completely new to me, I have no previous experience with the subject. My work experience before this stemmed from a place of interest and convenience. Seeing life from different points of views is so important to me and I love to try new things. This led my work experience to accommodate what sort of life I was trying to live. At 18 I drove 34 hours to Colorado for a job I signed up for online. I worked out there for 14 months and it changed my life. Later I'd drive to North Carolina to work in Black Mountain. I worked in Yosemite National Park. In five years I was a life-guard, camp counselor high-ropes instructor, server, backroom associate, market team member, delivery driver, hike guide, archery instructor, the list goes on.

Where I'm Headed

No matter what I am doing I always have a plan on what is next. Yes, I am career focused but nothing matters more to me than my personal happiness and life experience. Not everything is about money, that is just a source to help us do what we actually want to do. I always want to be on the move, constant does not work for me. So the plan is to move to New York City in September after graduation. There I will job search and build a life, until its time to move again.

Family

I love meeting new people and creating new relationships. Over the years I have met hundreds of awesome individuals who I became super close with. Most of the time when I was on an adventure, most of the other people I'd interact with where in the same situation as me. We become each others support system and "family". A lot of these people will always hold a spot in my heart but it is hard to keep in contact when living so far. But one thing that has always been a constant is my hometown Peabody. Traveling made me realize that no matter where you go, no matter how far you are on a map, there will only be one dot on this planet where everything that actually matters exists. Peabody holds my blood family, parents, two sisters and two brothers. Then also my seven best friends who I have had since second grade and continue to talk to every single day. Traveling taught me so much and its what I love to do but it gave me the perspective that there is nothing better out there than what I have in Peabody.

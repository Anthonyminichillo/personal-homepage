Work Experience

A look back at the jobs that shaped me along the way.

El Portal Market — Yosemite National Park

Worked at a small market just outside Yosemite National Park, surrounded by some of the most incredible scenery I've ever seen. It taught me a lot about customer service in a remote setting and gave me a deep appreciation for the outdoors.

High Ropes Instructor — Black Mountain, NC

Led groups through high ropes courses, teaching trust, teamwork, and courage at height. This role pushed me to become a stronger leader and taught me how to stay calm and communicate clearly in high-pressure situations.

Camp Counselor — Peabody, MA

My first real job — leading activities for kids and being a positive role model for them over the summer. This is where I first realized how much I enjoy working with and leading people.

Lifeguard — Granby, CO

Spent a summer lifeguarding out in Colorado, combining my love for the outdoors with a job that required constant focus and responsibility for others' safety.

Server — Cheesecake Factory, Boston

Currently working as a server in Boston, balancing a fast-paced restaurant environment with grad school. It's taught me a lot about time management and staying composed under pressure.
